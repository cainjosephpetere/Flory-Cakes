FLORY CAKES WEBSITE

This is a static consultation website for Flory Cakes. It does not have a shopping cart, checkout, or online payments.

HOW TO VIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the flory-cakes-website folder.
3. Double-click index.html to view the website in your browser.

FILES
- index.html: website content and sections
- styles.css: layout, colours, typography, and responsive styles
- script.js: mobile navigation and consultation form behaviour
- images/: logo and cake photos

WHATSAPP CONSULTATION
The consultation form and WhatsApp buttons open a chat with +27 84 281 2497. The form prepares a message from the visitor's details; the visitor must press Send in WhatsApp.

EDITING
You can edit the text and page sections in index.html. Change colours near the top of styles.css inside :root. Replace photos in the images folder and update their filenames in index.html if needed.

LOCATION AND HOURS
The site currently displays: Sunbird Dr N, Cape Town, 7580, South Africa; Rondevallei. Opening hours are shown as 09:00–18:00 every day, based on the information provided.
